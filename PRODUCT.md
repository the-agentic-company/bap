# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Bap is used by people who delegate work to agents across connected company tools. They move between conversations, Coworker configuration, approvals, run history, and integration settings, often during a focused workday where speed, clarity, and confidence matter more than novelty.

## Product Purpose

Bap is an agentic operating system for building and running agents across connected company tools. It gives a Workspace a dependable place to configure access, start and inspect agent work, and understand what happened. Success means users can delegate real work quickly while retaining clear control over tools, identity, authorization, and runtime state.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and accountable. Bap should feel like a durable operations console: technically capable without terminal cosplay, friendly without becoming playful, and polished without looking ornamental.

## Anti-references

Bap should not look like a generic AI SaaS dashboard, a theatrical automation canvas, or a marketing page transplanted into an authenticated product. Avoid purple-blue gradients, decorative glass effects, oversized metrics, excessive card grids, vague product language, and motion that does not explain a state change.

## Design Principles

1. Make agency accountable: show ownership, authorization, state, and consequences where decisions are made.
2. Keep the operator in flow: use familiar controls, compact layouts, and progressive disclosure for technical detail.
3. Preserve exact product language: domain terms should remain consistent across the web app, CLI, runtime, and documentation.
4. Prefer quiet confidence: hierarchy should come from typography, spacing, borders, and clear copy before decoration.
5. Surface degraded states honestly: connection failures, missing permissions, and runtime warnings should remain visible and actionable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, full keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, and accessible names for icon-only actions. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep state transitions short and functional. New product UI should meet WCAG 2.1 AA expectations.
